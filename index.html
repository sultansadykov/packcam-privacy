PackCam for ShipBots — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

PackCam for ShipBots ("the extension") is an internal tool used on ShipBots warehouse packing stations.

What the extension handles. On shipping.shiphero.com only, the extension reads the address of the

order page (to get the order id) and notices clicks on the "Complete order" and "Print label" buttons.

Where the data goes. These events are sent only to the PackCam recorder running on the same

computer, at http://127.0.0.1:47800. The extension does not send any data to the internet, to

ShipBots, to the developer or to any third party.

What the extension does not do. It does not collect personal information, passwords, payment

data, customer data, page content or browsing history of other websites. It does not use cookies or

analytics. It does not sell or share data.

Storage. If the local recorder is temporarily unavailable, up to 1000 recent events are kept in the

browser's local extension storage on that computer until they are delivered.
